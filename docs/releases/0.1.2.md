# SalaryKitten 0.1.2

## New
- **Tuck into the screen edge**: drag the pet to the left or right edge and she slides in, peeking out with half her body. Hover to bring her back out; she also pops out on her own when she has something to say, and stays docked after a restart.

## Fixed
- macOS: the pet can now be dragged all the way up to the menu bar (she used to stop about 190 px below the top). When a speech bubble or the details card needs room at the top, she steps down for a moment and goes back up.
- macOS: the first click or drag on the pet while another app was active did nothing; it now works right away.
- A pet that was partly off-screen when you quit no longer jumps back to the default corner on the next launch.

## Install
- **macOS** (Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later): `SalaryKitten-mac.dmg` — drag SalaryKitten to Applications. Signed and notarized.
- **Windows** (x64, Windows 10/11): `SalaryKitten-win-Setup.exe`. Not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen appears, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Installed apps update themselves (right-click the pet → 检查更新).
